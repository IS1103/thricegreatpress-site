Questions

How long does delivery take?

Each copy is printed and bound for you when you order, which takes about 3–5 business days, and then ships with tracking: about 6–9 business days to the UK and 12–14 business days to the US.

Are these the complete texts?

Yes. Nothing is abridged or paraphrased. The only thing we leave out is scholarly apparatus: G. R. S. Mead’s footnotes are omitted from Volume II; his translation is complete.

Which translations do you use?

The Kybalion is the original 1908 English text. The Corpus Hermeticum and The Perfect Sermon are G. R. S. Mead’s translation of 1906. Volume III reproduces The Mirror of Alchimy (London, 1597) in its original spelling, the Emerald Tablet in the translations of Isaac Newton, Sigismund Bacstrom and H. P. Blavatsky, and The Book of Lambspring in A. E. Waite’s translation of 1893.

Is the gold on the cover foil?

No. The gold frame and engraving are printed, not foil-stamped. The cover is a matte printed hardcover (case-wrap) in deep emerald.

What is the digital edition?

A PDF of the same typeset pages, including every engraving, sent to your email within 24 hours of purchase. It is included free with every hardcover.

Do you ship to my country?

For now we ship to the United States and the United Kingdom, free. For other countries, write to us before ordering.
