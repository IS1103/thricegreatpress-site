Privacy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you buy, our checkout partner Whop collects your name, email, payment details and shipping address. We receive your name, email and shipping address so that your book can be printed and delivered. We never see or store your card details.

How we use it

Your shipping address is passed to our print partner only to print and deliver your order. We use your email to send order and delivery updates, and to answer your messages. We do not sell your information.

Analytics and advertising

This site may use advertising pixels (for example Meta) to measure which advertisements lead to purchases. You can block these with your browser’s privacy settings.
